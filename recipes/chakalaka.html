Chakalaka

Country: South Africa

Cooking Time: 35 minutes

Difficulty: Easy

Servings: 4

About This Dish

Chakalaka is a spicy vegetable relish from South Africa. It is commonly made with onions, peppers, carrots, tomatoes, and beans. It is often served with pap, bread, grilled meat, or other meals.

Ingredients

  • 1 onion
  • 2 carrots, grated
  • 1 red bell pepper
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 cup cooked baked beans
  • 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
  • 1 teaspoon curry powder
  • 1 garlic clove
  • Chili pepper to taste
  • Salt to taste

Cooking Instructions

Step 1: Prepare the Vegetables

Wash and chop the onion, tomatoes, and pepper. Grate the carrots and mince the garlic.

Step 2: Fry the Onion

Heat the oil in a pan and add the onion and garlic. Cook until the onion softens.

Step 3: Add the Vegetables

Add the carrots, peppers, and tomatoes. Stir and cook until the vegetables begin to soften.

Step 4: Add Beans and Seasoning

Add the baked beans, curry powder, chili pepper, and salt. Stir and simmer for several minutes.

Step 5: Serve

Remove the pan from the heat. Serve Chakalaka warm or at room temperature with pap, bread, or grilled meat.
