South African Chakalaka

Country: South Africa

Cooking Time: 35 minutes

Difficulty: Easy

Servings: 4

About the Dish

Chakalaka is a spicy vegetable relish commonly enjoyed in South Africa. It is usually made with onions, peppers, carrots, tomatoes, and beans.

Chakalaka can be served hot or cold and is often eaten with pap, bread, or grilled meat.

Ingredients

  • 1 large onion
  • 2 carrots
  • 1 red bell pepper
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1 cup cooked beans
  • 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
  • 1 teaspoon curry powder
  • 1 fresh chili pepper
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Cooking Instructions

Step 1: Prepare the Vegetables

Chop the onion, tomatoes, bell pepper, and chili. Grate the carrots.

Step 2: Cook the Onion

Heat oil in a pan and cook the onion until softened.

Step 3: Add Vegetables

Add the bell pepper, chili, carrots, and curry powder. Stir and cook for several minutes.

Step 4: Add Tomatoes and Beans

Add the tomatoes and cooked beans. Simmer until the vegetables are tender and the mixture thickens.

Step 5: Serve

Season to taste and serve warm or cold with pap, bread, or grilled meat.
