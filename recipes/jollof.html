Ghanaian Jollof Rice

Country: Ghana

Cooking Time: 60 minutes

Difficulty: Medium

Servings: 4

About This Dish

Jollof Rice is one of the most popular dishes in Ghana and other West African countries. It is made by cooking rice in a tomato-based sauce with peppers, onions, and spices. In Ghana, it is commonly served at parties, celebrations, and family gatherings.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups long-grain rice
  • 4 tomatoes, blended
  • 2 tablespoons tomato paste
  • 1 large onion
  • 1 red bell pepper
  • 1 scotch bonnet pepper (optional)
  • 3 tablespoons vegetable oil
  • 2 cups chicken or vegetable stock
  • 1 teaspoon curry powder
  • 1 teaspoon dried thyme
  • Salt to taste

Cooking Instructions

Step 1: Prepare the Ingredients

Wash the rice and set it aside. Blend the tomatoes and peppers. Chop the onion into small pieces.

Step 2: Prepare the Tomato Sauce

Heat the oil in a pot and fry the onions. Add the tomato paste and cook for a few minutes. Add the blended tomatoes and peppers, then cook until the sauce thickens.

Step 3: Add the Seasoning

Add the curry powder, thyme, salt, and stock. Stir everything together and allow the mixture to simmer.

Step 4: Cook the Rice

Add the washed rice and stir gently. Cover the pot and cook on low heat until the rice becomes tender. Add a little water if needed.

Step 5: Serve

Fluff the rice with a fork and serve while warm. Jollof Rice can be served with chicken, salad, or fried plantain.
