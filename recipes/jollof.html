Ghanaian Jollof Rice

Country: Ghana

Cooking Time: 60 minutes

Difficulty: Medium

Servings: 4

About the Dish

Jollof Rice is one of the most popular dishes in West Africa. It is prepared by cooking rice in a flavorful tomato sauce with peppers, onions, and spices.

In Ghana, Jollof Rice is commonly served at parties, celebrations, and family gatherings. It can be enjoyed with chicken, fish, or vegetables.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups long-grain rice
  • 3 medium tomatoes
  • 2 tablespoons tomato paste
  • 1 large onion
  • 1 red bell pepper
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 1 teaspoon ginger
  • 3 tablespoons vegetable oil
  • 2 cups chicken or vegetable stock
  • 1 teaspoon curry powder
  • 1 teaspoon thyme
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Cooking Instructions

Step 1: Prepare the Vegetables

Blend the tomatoes, bell pepper, garlic, and part of the onion until smooth.

Step 2: Prepare the Sauce

Heat oil in a pot and fry the remaining chopped onion. Add tomato paste and cook for a few minutes.

Step 3: Add the Seasonings

Pour in the blended vegetables. Add ginger, curry powder, thyme, salt, and pepper. Cook until the sauce thickens.

Step 4: Cook the Rice

Add the washed rice and stock. Stir, cover the pot, and cook on low heat until the rice is tender.

Step 5: Serve

Fluff the rice with a fork and serve with chicken, fish, or vegetables.
