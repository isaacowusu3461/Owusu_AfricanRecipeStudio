Nigerian Egusi Soup

Country: Nigeria

Cooking Time: 55 minutes

Difficulty: Medium

Servings: 4

About the Dish

Egusi Soup is a traditional Nigerian dish made with ground melon seeds. The seeds give the soup a rich flavor and thick texture.

The soup is commonly prepared with leafy vegetables, peppers, and meat or fish. It is often served with pounded yam, eba, or fufu.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup ground egusi seeds
  • 2 cups meat or vegetable stock
  • 1 onion
  • 2 fresh peppers
  • 2 tablespoons palm oil
  • 2 cups chopped spinach
  • 1 cup cooked meat or fish
  • 1 teaspoon ground crayfish, optional
  • Salt and seasoning to taste

Cooking Instructions

Step 1: Prepare the Ingredients

Chop the onion and peppers. Wash the spinach and prepare the cooked meat or fish.

Step 2: Cook the Base

Heat palm oil in a pot. Add the onion and peppers and cook for a few minutes.

Step 3: Add the Egusi

Mix the ground egusi with a little water to form a paste. Add it to the pot and allow it to cook briefly.

Step 4: Add Stock and Vegetables

Add the stock, meat or fish, and seasonings. Simmer until the egusi is cooked, then add the spinach.

Step 5: Serve

Cook for a few more minutes and serve the soup with your preferred swallow.
