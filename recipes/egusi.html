Egusi Soup

Country: Nigeria

Cooking Time: 55 minutes

Difficulty: Medium

Servings: 4

About This Dish

Egusi Soup is a popular Nigerian dish prepared with ground melon seeds. It has a thick texture and is usually cooked with vegetables, peppers, and meat or fish. It is commonly eaten with pounded yam, eba, or fufu.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup ground egusi seeds
  • 2 cups chopped spinach
  • 1 onion
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1 red bell pepper
  • 1 scotch bonnet pepper (optional)
  • 3 tablespoons palm oil
  • 2 cups meat or vegetable stock
  • Cooked meat or fish (optional)
  • Salt and seasoning to taste

Cooking Instructions

Step 1: Prepare the Ingredients

Blend the tomatoes, peppers, and onion. Wash and chop the spinach. Set the ground egusi seeds aside.

Step 2: Cook the Pepper Mixture

Heat the palm oil in a pot. Add the blended mixture and cook for several minutes.

Step 3: Add the Egusi

Mix the ground egusi with a little water to form a paste. Add it to the pot and allow it to cook until it begins to thicken.

Step 4: Add Stock and Vegetables

Add the stock, seasoning, and cooked meat or fish if using. Stir gently and add the spinach. Allow the soup to simmer until everything is cooked.

Step 5: Serve

Taste and adjust the seasoning. Serve the soup warm with pounded yam, eba, or another preferred side.
