East African Chapati

Region: East Africa

Cooking Time: 45 minutes

Difficulty: Easy

Servings: 4

About the Dish

Chapati is a popular flatbread enjoyed in several East African countries, including Kenya, Uganda, and Tanzania.

It is made using flour, water, oil, and salt. Chapati is commonly served with beans, vegetables, or meat stew.

Ingredients

  • 3 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup warm water, approximately
  • 3 tablespoons vegetable oil
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • Extra flour for rolling
  • Extra oil for cooking

Cooking Instructions

Step 1: Prepare the Dough

Combine flour and salt in a bowl. Add water and oil gradually, mixing until a soft dough forms.

Step 2: Knead the Dough

Knead the dough until smooth. Cover it and allow it to rest for about 15 minutes.

Step 3: Divide and Roll

Divide the dough into equal portions. Roll each portion into a thin circle on a lightly floured surface.

Step 4: Cook the Chapati

Heat a frying pan over medium heat. Cook each chapati on both sides, adding a little oil until golden brown.

Step 5: Serve

Remove from the pan and serve warm with stew, beans, or vegetables.
