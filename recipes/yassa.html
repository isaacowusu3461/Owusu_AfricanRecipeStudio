Senegalese Chicken Yassa

Country: Senegal

Cooking Time: 75 minutes

Difficulty: Medium

Servings: 4

About the Dish

Chicken Yassa is a traditional dish associated with Senegal and other parts of West Africa. It is known for its combination of onions, lemon, and mustard.

The chicken is usually marinated before cooking and served with rice.

Ingredients

  • 4 chicken pieces
  • 3 large onions
  • 2 lemons
  • 2 tablespoons mustard
  • 3 tablespoons vegetable oil
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 1 cup chicken stock
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • Cooked rice for serving

Cooking Instructions

Step 1: Prepare the Marinade

Slice the onions and mix them with lemon juice, mustard, garlic, salt, and pepper.

Step 2: Marinate the Chicken

Coat the chicken with the marinade and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes.

Step 3: Brown the Chicken

Heat oil in a pan and brown the chicken pieces on both sides. Remove them temporarily from the pan.

Step 4: Cook the Onion Sauce

Add the marinated onions to the pan and cook until softened. Return the chicken, add stock, and simmer until the chicken is fully cooked.

Step 5: Serve

Serve the chicken with the onion sauce over cooked rice.
