Chicken Yassa

Country: Senegal

Cooking Time: 75 minutes

Difficulty: Medium

Servings: 4

About This Dish

Chicken Yassa is a traditional dish associated with Senegal and the wider Senegambian region. It is known for its lemon and onion sauce. The chicken is marinated with lemon, mustard, and spices before cooking. It is usually served with rice.

Ingredients

  • 4 chicken pieces
  • 3 large onions
  • 2 lemons
  • 2 tablespoons mustard
  • 3 tablespoons vegetable oil
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 cup chicken stock
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • Cooked rice for serving

Cooking Instructions

Step 1: Marinate the Chicken

Mix lemon juice, mustard, garlic, salt, and pepper. Coat the chicken with the mixture and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes.

Step 2: Prepare the Onions

Slice the onions into thin pieces. Set them aside while preparing the chicken.

Step 3: Brown the Chicken

Heat oil in a pan and brown the chicken pieces on both sides. Remove them from the pan.

Step 4: Cook the Sauce

Add the onions to the pan and cook until soft. Add the stock and return the chicken to the pan. Cover and simmer until the chicken is fully cooked.

Step 5: Serve

Taste the sauce and adjust the seasoning. Serve the chicken and onion sauce over cooked rice.
