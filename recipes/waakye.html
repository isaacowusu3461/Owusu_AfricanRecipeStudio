Waakye

Country: Ghana

Cooking Time: 90 minutes

Difficulty: Medium

Servings: 4

About This Dish

Waakye is a traditional Ghanaian dish made by cooking rice and beans together. It is a popular street food in Ghana and is often eaten for breakfast or lunch. It is commonly served with stew, boiled eggs, fried plantain, and other side dishes.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups rice
  • 1 cup dried black-eyed peas
  • 5 cups water, plus more if needed
  • 3 dried sorghum leaves (optional)
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • Stew for serving
  • Boiled eggs (optional)
  • Fried plantain (optional)

Cooking Instructions

Step 1: Wash the Beans

Rinse the black-eyed peas with clean water and remove any unwanted particles.

Step 2: Cook the Beans

Place the beans in a pot with water. Add the dried sorghum leaves if available. Cook until the beans begin to soften.

Step 3: Add the Rice

Wash the rice and add it to the pot. Stir gently to combine it with the beans.

Step 4: Cook Together

Add salt and enough water to cook the rice. Cover the pot and cook on low heat until both the rice and beans are tender.

Step 5: Serve

Remove the sorghum leaves before serving. Serve the Waakye with stew, boiled eggs, fried plantain, or your preferred sides.
